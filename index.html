About

I'm an AI engineer who builds the plumbing behind language models — retrieval pipelines, vector search, and tool-calling infrastructure — rather than just the prompts on top. At Adela Software, I work on a production RAG platform that answers natural-language tax-form queries using hybrid search (BM25 + dense embeddings) and cross-encoder reranking, backed by Qdrant and ChromaDB.

Before that, I spent time on the other side of the AI stack: training GANs for synthetic tabular data and fraud detection, running exploratory data analysis across finance and healthcare datasets, and shipping a Model Context Protocol tool server with ten-plus callable tools. I like systems that are explainable and deterministic where it matters, and generative where it counts.
